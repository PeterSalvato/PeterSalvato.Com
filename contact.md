# Working Together

I figure out what a project is actually supposed to be, then build the design, engineering, and UX/IA so it stays that way through execution.

The first conversation is diagnostic. You tell me what you're building and what isn't working. I listen for the gap between what you intend and what's actually coming through: in the brand, the platform, the architecture, the copy. Sometimes the problem is what you think it is. Sometimes it's structural and nobody's named it yet.

Good fit: your brand looks professional but doesn't feel like you. Your product is good but strangers can't tell. Your team keeps making reasonable decisions that slowly contradict each other. Your platform works but the experience feels off and nobody can say why. The pieces are good individually but don't add up.
