A student in my classroom hears three instructions and processes the first one. The rest drops. I learned to read what each person could actually hold, then design the task around that.

I have been doing the same thing in every room since. Enterprise platforms, brand systems, AI tools, the household breakfast schedule. The material is different each time. The first move is the same: figure out what the system on the receiving end actually needs, and build the structure to meet it.

I wrote up the thinking as [accommodation design](/practice/accommodation-design/). I built the process as [FormWork](/systems/formwork/). This site was compiled from three years of my own conversations using both.
