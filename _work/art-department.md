Source images and JSON manifests go in. Print-ready documents come out. No Adobe, no cloud, no subscription. The entire chain runs in the terminal.

Three template types run through the engine now. [Photogeography](/practice/photogeography/) produces 11x17 tabloid posters with EXIF-driven metadata. [Versograms](/practice/versograms/) produces 8.5x11 posters with per-song display fonts, AI-generated imagery, and lyrics. Deep Cuts (a book in progress on DJ methodology) produces 6x9 book spreads with facing pages and margin notes.

The Art Department handles layout and data. [PressWorks](/practice/pressworks/) handles ink. Same manifest, same template, same output every time.
