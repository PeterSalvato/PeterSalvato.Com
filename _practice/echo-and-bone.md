The same four patterns kept showing up across unrelated projects. A brand identity, a narrative structure, a household routine. Hero, Shadow, Wise Old Man, Trickster. I wasn't looking for mythology. I was looking at why certain structures hold weight across completely different materials.

Stoicism is the first test case. Three prints. Each one pairs a Stoic concept with a single visual symbol. The constraint: the visual element encodes the same content as the text, through a different channel. Type and symbol form a single diagram.

The same structural logic shows up in the [Order of the Ætherwright's](/practice/order-of-the-aetherwright/) glyph system and the character design in [New City](/practice/new-city/). The taxonomy gives you the structural logic. The specific assembly is where it becomes something you can build with.
