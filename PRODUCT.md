# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design leaders, creative directors, and technical decision-makers evaluating a practitioner for consulting engagements. People who do serious work in adjacent fields (design, engineering, AI governance) and recognize depth when they see it. Secondary: peers discovering the methodology through essays or brand walks.

They arrive from LinkedIn, search, or a direct referral. They're assessing credibility and fit, not shopping features. The site is the proof before the conversation starts.

## Product Purpose

petersalvato.com is a practitioner's world, not a portfolio. It demonstrates coherence across 27 years of cross-domain practice (construction, print, special education, enterprise software, AI) through published methodology, case studies, brand readings, and essays. The site itself runs on the governance it documents: voice protocols, evaluation lenses, design systems.

Success: a stranger knows who Peter is and how he thinks before reading a single case study. The room communicates identity before anyone explains anything.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, direct, warm.

"Bauhaus at a dive bar." Structural discipline with human temperature. The expertise shows through what's described, not how it's framed. Blue collar, think intellectual. The genre: cyberdeck builder, systems engineer, zine maker, practitioner who reads philosophy and grows his own food.

Positioning peers: Jon Contino, Aaron Draplin. Designers whose brands are inseparable from who they are.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio templates (Squarespace creative portfolio, Cargo collective defaults)
- SaaS marketing pages (gradient heroes, metric cards, "trusted by" logo bars)
- AI consultant sites (purple gradients, robot imagery, "the future of" language)
- Minimalist-for-minimalism's-sake (white void, no personality, interchangeable)
- Design agency sites that perform creativity without showing real work

## Design Principles

1. **The room before the content.** The ambient frame (typography, color, texture, density) communicates identity before anyone reads body copy. A stranger arriving through any page lands in the same world.
2. **Show, don't describe.** The site runs on the methodology it documents. The coherence is demonstrated, not claimed.
3. **Practitioner register.** First person, direct, specific. No hedging, no performing, no flex. The reader decides if the work is impressive.
4. **Earned density.** Flagship pages get full ornament. Terminal pages thin out. Nothing is decorated that hasn't earned it.
5. **The stranger and the initiate.** Immediately legible layer (sidebar, tagline, navigation) establishes the primary identity. Cryptic layer (codex, glyphs, altitude markers) adds depth for people who stay. The legible layer stands alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Peter led a full WCAG compliance initiative at his day job. The site should meet the standard he sets for clients. Reduced motion respected. Color contrast verified. No reliance on color alone for meaning.
