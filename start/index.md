# Start Here

These are tools you paste into your AI assistant. They change how the conversation works. Your AI starts remembering where your thinking was, breaking big tasks into pieces you can actually do, and keeping your voice instead of flattening it into something generic.

Each one takes about a minute to set up. No code. No account. You paste a text file into your AI's instructions and the tool is live across every conversation.

I built and tested these with [Claude](https://claude.ai). Each lesson walks you through setting it up there. The instructions are plain text, so they'll work in other AI tools that support custom instructions, but Claude is what I use and what I can vouch for.
